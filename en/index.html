A day with JETZT.

Morning

Three questions sort your day.

How much energy have you got? Any meetings? Anything urgent? JETZT. builds your day from that and only shows you what's realistic today.

  • Energy first. On a slow day the easy things move up, on a good day the big ones.
  • Not in the mood? Tap Skip and the app won't ask again.

During the day

Write it the way you'd say it.

Type “Dentist tomorrow at noon” and the date is set. Everything goes into the list, but the card only shows what fits right now.

  • Whole lists at once. Paste your notes and JETZT. turns them into separate tasks.
  • Your shopping list on the card. Milk, bread and apples sit right on the card, and you tick them off in the store.

Apple Intelligence, right on your iPhoneYour tasks don't leave the device for detection.

Evening

Only what you got done counts here.

The stats show your week and what you finished today. Skip a day and you don't start over from zero.

  • A sentence, not a grade. After three tasks it says “Good on you for getting started”, not a percentage.
  • Wrap up the day. In the evening, one tap moves what's left to tomorrow and you can close the app.

What the research says.

  • With ADHD, self-regulation develops more slowly, by about 30 percent according to Russell A. Barkley. Putting things off is not a character flaw.
  • Starting is the hardest part. A small first step helps more than any amount of motivation, which is why the timer starts at two minutes.
  • If you only see one task, you don't have to choose between many. With ADHD, that choosing is what drains you most.
